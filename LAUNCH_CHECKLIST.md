- [ ] Domain bought and `NEXT_PUBLIC_SITE_URL` set (canonicals, sitemap, schema depend on it)
- [ ] Didin's DLD / RERA **BRN** (`NEXT_PUBLIC_DIDIN_BRN`) and Triad **ORN** and **trade licence**
